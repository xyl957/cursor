# Monday Night Football

- Offensive Line → Defensive Line
- Quarterback → Pass rushers (Defensive Ends + Outside Linebackers)
- Running Back → Linebackers
- Wide Receiver → Cornerback
- Tight End → Linebacker or Safety

## Chiefs Offense vs. Broncos Defense — September 14, 2026

- **Offensive line → Defensive line:** Broncos advantage. Chiefs left tackle Josh Simmons is out, and rookie Kahlil Benson is expected to replace him.
- **Patrick Mahomes → Pass rush:** Broncos advantage. Denver recorded an NFL-leading 68 sacks last season. Mahomes is returning from an ACL tear, although Denver is missing Jonathon Cooper.
- **Kenneth Walker III → Linebackers:** Slight Broncos advantage. Denver allowed only 3.9 yards per rush last season.
- **Receivers → Cornerbacks:** Slight Broncos advantage. Rashee Rice and Xavier Worthy are dangerous, but Pat Surtain II anchors an excellent secondary.
- **Travis Kelce → Linebackers/Safeties:** Chiefs advantage. Kelce and Noah Gray offer Kansas City its most favorable quick-passing matchup.

**Overall:** Denver's defense has the matchup advantage. Kansas City's best approach is quick passes, screens, Kelce over the middle, and Walker on early downs—avoiding obvious passing situations.
